How an order works

Most jobs go from first call to finished product in four steps.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Call, WhatsApp photos and rough sizes, or bring the vehicle or item to the workshop.

  2. We measure and quote

    We help you pick a fabric, colour and finish, then give you a written quote.

  3. We cut and stitch

    Your product is patterned and made by hand in our eMalahleni workshop.

  4. Fitted and ready

    Collect it, or we arrange fitting for enclosures, canopies and larger jobs.

Owned and run by Chris Dreyer

Chris has more than 16 years' experience in the tarpaulin, canvas, mining and marketing industries. That mix is why AC Canvas can make a pool net for a family home and a mine-spec seat cover for a haul truck in the same week.

We believe our people are what set us apart, so we invest in a motivated, skilled team who treat each order as their own work. Everything we supply to the public, transport companies and the mining industry is custom designed and made.
